How it works

From a paper prescription to today’s list

Three steps, and you stay in charge of each one.

  1. 1

    Snap a prescription

    Take a photo of your prescription, or upload one you already have.

  2. 2

    Check what it read

    Ayoreni drafts the medicines and doctor details for you. Nothing is saved as fact until you confirm it.

  3. 3

    See today’s medicines

    Today shows what’s due now and what’s done. You mark each dose taken yourself.
